## 3D models

No third-party 3D model ships with this project: the car, the track and the
pit-stop diagram are procedural. Two CC-BY candidates were downloaded and
evaluated before that decision was taken, and both must be attributed even though
neither is shipped:

| File | Author | Licence | Reason rejected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `open-cockpit-design.glb` | Niall McCaffrey (via Poly Pizza) | CC-BY 3.0 | 267 anonymous `group<random>` meshes - not separable into named parts, so per-part hotspots and the exploded view are impossible |
| `car-model-formula.glb` | Ignition Labs (via Poly Pizza) | CC-BY 3.0 | The model is a closed-wheel road car (Lamborghini Aventador) in millimetre units - wrong subject |

See `public/assets/models/README.md` for the full search and evaluation record.

## Audio

No audio files ship with this project. Every sound is synthesised at runtime with
the Web Audio API, so there is no third-party audio licence to honour.

## Photography that could not be obtained

The image set was curated as 50 picks across hero, car detail, night, pit-lane,
driver, tyre/carbon, gallery and background categories. 26 are shipped. The
remaining 24 could not be downloaded because the Wikimedia image CDN returned
HTTP 429 (rate limited) for the full duration of the fetch window. Every affected
section falls back to a labelled placeholder rather than a broken image, and
re-running `npm run assets` retries the missing picks.

Two Poly Haven texture maps (metal_plate, aerial_beach_01) were also not
retrieved, so 2 environment entries are recorded but absent.
The scene only ever loads the five HDRIs listed above; the missing textures have
no licence obligation because no bytes were received.
